FAQ

What documents do I need?

Valid driving license and a government ID (Aadhar/Passport). Security deposit may apply per vendor.

Is fuel included?

Fuel is generally not included. Bikes are provided with minimal fuel; please refuel as needed.

What if I exceed free kilometers?

Extra km will be charged per vendor tariff at return.
