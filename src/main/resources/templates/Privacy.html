1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly and information automatically collected when you use our services.

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, profile photo.
  • Payment information: payment method token (we do not store full card numbers when using external processors).
  • Ride details: pickup & drop locations, timestamps, driver details and ratings.
  • Device & usage data: IP address, device identifiers, browser type, cookies, and analytics data.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the service (matching riders and drivers, processing payments, trip history).
  • To communicate with you about bookings, changes, promotions, and account notices.
  • To improve the service through analytics and product development.
  • To prevent fraud and ensure safety (identity verification, background checks, SOS features).

3. When we share information

We never sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers: payment processors, cloud hosting, analytics providers—only to perform tasks on our behalf.
  • Drivers: limited ride details (pickup/drop, contact) necessary to complete your trip.
  • Legal requests: to comply with laws, respond to legal processes, or protect rights and safety.

4. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, analyze trends, and provide personalized content.

  • Essential cookies: required for site functionality and security.
  • Performance & analytics: help us understand how the site is used.
  • Advertising: used only if you opt in, to show relevant offers.

You can control cookies via your browser settings. Disabling cookies may limit features.

5. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No system is 100% secure; if a breach occurs we will notify affected users as required by law.

6. Your privacy choices & rights

  • Access & portability: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications anytime via links in emails or account settings.

To exercise rights, contact us at the email below. We may need to verify your identity before processing requests.
